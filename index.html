Before you order

How to use an OopBuy spreadsheet

From a find to an informed choice

An OopBuy spreadsheet organizes product links so you can compare options without losing the original listing. This page provides a CAFANS selection and a practical review workflow.

  1. Choose an exact option. Record the product, color, size, quantity and source URL. Similar images can represent different variants.
  2. Review the current listing. Open CAFANS and inspect the available buying options. Confirm that the intended item can be purchased through your chosen agent.
  3. Check the terms before paying. Ask about stock, seller delivery charges, service fees and return conditions for that item.
  4. Review warehouse photos when available. Match the received item to your ordered option and request close-ups or measurements for unresolved questions.
  5. Compare the complete shipping quote. Check the destination, parcel dimensions, chargeable weight and any excluded charges before selecting a route.

Useful answers

OopBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this an official OopBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide. Its catalog selection comes from CAFANS, and the two main buttons direct you to CAFANS. This page does not represent OopBuy or process orders.

Are the displayed products real catalog entries?

The eight product names, photos and estimated USD prices were read from the CAFANS homepage on November 19, 2026. The selection is stored in this page and does not automatically synchronize. Check the current source listing before purchasing.

Do the prices include international shipping?

They are estimated item amounts from the source catalog. Do not treat them as delivered prices. Confirm shipping, payment fees, taxes and any other applicable charges separately.

Can every CAFANS product be ordered through OopBuy?

Do not assume universal availability. Check the current product page and buying options, then confirm item eligibility and the selected variant with your agent.

Does this spreadsheet include verified QC photos?

No independent QC verification is claimed here. The displayed images come from product listings. If warehouse inspection photos become available for your order, compare them with your chosen variant and request clarification before shipping.

How should I choose clothing or shoe sizes?

Use the specific listing’s measurement guidance. Compare equivalent measurements with a garment or pair that fits you, keep units consistent and ask about missing information. A familiar size label alone is insufficient.

Why can a shipping quote change?

Quotes can depend on the packed parcel, destination, route and chargeable weight. Ask which measurements and charges the quote uses, and compare routes using the same parcel details.
